WebP & Responsive Images

This example offers WebP sources first, JPEG fallbacks, and multiple widths so the browser can choose an appropriate image.

A landscape at the right size

How it works

  • <source type="image/webp"> offers WebP to browsers that support it.
  • The nested JPEG <img> remains the fallback and also supplies the alternative text.
  • srcset and sizes let the browser select an image candidate based on viewport and pixel density.
  • Open the browser's Network panel to inspect the selected format and transferred image size.
