What to notice

  • width and height reserve the image's aspect ratio before download.
  • loading="lazy" is useful for below-the-fold images; avoid lazy loading the main hero image.
  • decoding="async" lets the browser decode images without blocking the next paint.
  • The page's external script uses defer and reports image loading events below.
