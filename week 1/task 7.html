Tsuivan (Mongolian Food)

What You Need

  • 2 cups of flour
  • Meat, potatoes, and carrots
  • Mongolian seasoning
  • Sesame oil
  • Egg and kimchi, if you like

Steps

  1. Cook the meat and let it cool slightly.
  2. Add the vegetables.
  3. Warning: use medium heat when frying everything.
  4. Add the noodles.
  5. Add salt and sesame oil, then mix everything together.
